AI vendors change their pricing, rate limits and terms all the time, and most of those changes come with no announcement.

So I put 46 of those public pages from 10 vendors into git. Every change becomes a dated, reviewable diff. Six months of history are rebuilt from the Wayback Machine.

A few from the archive:
- xAI text-to-speech went from $4.20 to $15.00 per 1M characters
- DeepSeek added peak-hour pricing at 2x
- Mistral's commercial terms replaced the right to object to updates with "stop using the products"
- Cursor's privacy policy dropped its "we do not sell or share personal data for behavioral advertising" line

If your team buys AI APIs, the RSS feed is a cheap way to see a contract change before your invoice does.
