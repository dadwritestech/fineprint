Snapshots of public, logged-out pages, normalized to markdown and committed to git. One-line summaries are written by a local LLM and can be wrong: every entry links the raw diff, which is the source of truth. History before the first live fetch is reconstructed from Wayback Machine captures sampled about weekly, so dates are "first seen in a capture", not exact change times.
